# Screen Visual Specifications
Home/Companion - question: where am I and where next? Reference: Nintendo Today + ZELDA NOTES. High intensity. Active game/next objective/progress are protagonists. One dominant Continue Journey action. No shortcut grid/dashboard.
Journey - what do I do now? Reference: ZELDA NOTES. Current step is protagonist. Contextual art and concise preparation; never embed full Pokedex/Collection.
Pokedex - what exists and what is missing? Reference: Pokemon HOME + Nintendo information hierarchy. Pokemon are protagonists. Expressive grid, artwork, number/type/status hierarchy; compact filters.
Game Dex - what exists/is missing here? Pokemon HOME game Dex + ZELDA NOTES progress. Compact region hero, registered/total and practical filters. Keep multi-region Dexes distinct.
Collection - what do I own? Pokemon HOME. Captured Pokemon are protagonists. Subtle shiny treatment; no duplicate Home dashboard.
Box - how did I organize Pokemon? Pokemon HOME storage. Preserve 6x5/30 slots. Calm surface and clear selection feedback.
Pokemon Detail - who is this Pokemon and what matters? Pokemon-first hero, type-derived atmosphere, content as chapters rather than unrelated card stacks.
Evolution Center - what/how can I evolve? Pokemon -> condition -> Pokemon graphical paths. Avoid text-only database presentation.
Capture Planner/Best Route - how do I obtain missing Pokemon? ZELDA NOTES navigation/progress. Route/timeline; capture -> evolution -> transfer -> trade/special -> unconfirmed. Never invent locations.
Universal Search - where can I find information? Nintendo Music discovery. Integrated search and grouped Pokemon/moves/abilities/items. Preserve debounce.
Settings - how is app configured? Nintendo Music/Nintendo Store restraint. Minimal intensity. Groups: Experience, Offline Content, Data/Storage, About.
